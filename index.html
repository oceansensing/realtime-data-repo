realtime-data-repo

The data service behind the C4PO ocean map: storms, gliders, USVs, Argo floats, currents, temperature, salinity, ice, wind and waves, refreshed through the hour. It is not a public API — the files under map/ exist to be read by the map, and their layout is that map's contract.

What is fresh, what is held, and why: status/status.json. The run's intent and outcome sit beside it as plan.json and receipt.json.

How it works, and why it is shaped the way it is: the repository README.
